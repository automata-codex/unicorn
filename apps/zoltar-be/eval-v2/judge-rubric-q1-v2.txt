### Rubric v2

Written 2026-10-10, after the judge check (unicorn `docs/eval-findings.md
§ S67`). It restates Rubric v1 as a checklist and adds three rulings v1 did
not make. Mark from the checklist; the table and the examples are for when
you are unsure.

Marks deck position only: where places and people are, and the routes between
them. Ground truth is the seeded `ship_layout` and the opening narration, not
the facts the Warden wrote during the session.

1. **Take the start from the table.** It comes from the session's history,
   even where the history is wrong.
2. **Find every phrase that puts a place or person on a deck, or moves someone
   between decks.** If there are none, mark _na_.
3. **Mark _fail_ if any of these is true:**
   - a place or person is on a different deck from the seeded layout. When no
     deck is named, work it out from the start
   - the route passes through, or returns to, the deck Danny starts on
   - "back up" or "back down" with no deck named would put the destination on
     the wrong deck, reading it as one deck up or down
   - a change of deck does not go by the ladder shaft
4. **Otherwise mark _pass_.**

A narration is never failed for the start it inherited, only for what it
narrates from there. One that names the right deck outright passes.

_error_ is not a mark: the turn threw and there is no narration.

#### Worked examples

_na_

- Turn 18, "You knock twice." No deck, no route, no change of position.

_pass_

- Turn 18, "The walk back to Mara's berth is short." A walk with no change of
  deck keeps the berth on mid-deck, where the layout has it.
- Turn 24, the mess is "one deck up" from Mara's berth. From the history's
  start on the lower deck that is mid-deck. It repeats the inherited error
  about the berth and is not failed for it.
- Turn 24, the destination is named outright as mid-deck, a short climb away.
- Turn 24, a narration that says the berths were on mid-deck all along and
  the earlier narration had them wrong, then puts Teo's bunk along the same
  deck. Mid-deck is named on the page.

_fail_: a place on the wrong deck

- Turn 18, "You head back down to the lower deck" to reach Mara's berth.
  Berths are on mid-deck.
- Turn 24, the mess is "two decks up" from Mara's berth. From the lower deck
  that is the upper deck.
- Turn 24, "Teo's bunk is just along this deck." From the history's start
  that is the lower deck, and no deck is named to say otherwise.

_fail_: the route passes through or returns to the starting deck

- Turn 14, "past mid-deck" on the way down, from a mid-deck start. The
  destination is right and the route is not possible.
- Turn 18, "You head back to mid-deck", from the cryo bay bulkhead. It has
  put him somewhere else first. *(new in v2)*
- Turn 18, "You head back up to mid-deck", from the same start.

_fail_: "back up" or "back down" with no deck named

- Turn 18, "You head back down" to Mara's berth, from the cryo bay bulkhead.
  Both are on mid-deck. It is not read as a figure of speech. *(new in v2)*
- Turn 18, "The walk back up from the cryo bay" to the berth. *(new in v2)*

_fail_: a change of deck that does not go by the ladder shaft

- Turn 29, "The walk down to the lower deck takes you past the ladder shaft",
  from the mess hall. The destination is right. *(new in v2)*
