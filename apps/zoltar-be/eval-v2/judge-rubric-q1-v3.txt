### Rubric v3

Written 2026-10-10, after the second judge check (unicorn
`docs/eval-findings.md § S69`). It is Rubric v2's checklist with two rules
changed. **This is the one to mark from.**

- **"Up" and "down" count only with a deck or the ladder shaft.** v2 read a
  bare "back down" as a deck change. The Warden prompt never tells the Warden
  to keep those words for deck changes, and people say "down to the kitchen"
  on one floor.
- **Turn 24 is marked from either start.** v1 and v2 failed a narration that
  got the layout right without comment, for not repeating the session's error.

Marks deck position only: where places and people are, and the routes between
them. Ground truth is the seeded `ship_layout` and the opening narration, not
the facts the Warden wrote during the session.

1. **Take the start from the table.** Turn 24 has two.
2. **Find every phrase that puts a place or person on a deck, or moves someone
   between decks.** "Up" or "down" is one only when the narration names a deck
   or the ladder shaft with it; otherwise ignore the word and mark the rest.
   If there are none, mark _na_.
3. **Mark _fail_ if any of these is true:**
   - a place or person is on a different deck from the seeded layout. When no
     deck is named, work it out from the start
   - the route passes through, or returns to, the deck Danny starts on
   - a change of deck does not go by the ladder shaft
4. **Otherwise mark _pass_.**

On turn 24, a narration passes if it is right from either start and fails only
if it is wrong from both. A narration that names the right deck outright
passes.

_error_ is not a mark: the turn threw and there is no narration.
