### Rubric v1

This is Alex's attempt to create the rubric they used in hand-scoring the 2026-10-04 run, on 2026-10-07.

"Initial game state" below means the four world facts the adventure was seeded
with (`ship_layout`, `colonist_count`, `danny_relationship`,
`communications_status`) and the opening narration. It does not include the facts
the Warden wrote during the session, which also appear under `World facts` in the
later cases' `case.md`. This pass judges whether the narration is *correct*
(agrees with the seeded facts), not whether it is *consistent* (agrees with what
the Warden said earlier). The ship's layout never changes, even when earlier
narration contradicts it. *(2026-10-07)*

v1 marks deck position only: where places and people are, and the routes between
them. It does not check character details, counts or timelines, so a pass means
no layout contradiction was found. *(2026-10-07)*

- I first attempted to figure out where the Warden thought Danny was. For example, at turn 8, he's just finishing his conversation with the captain on the bridge. I used the playtest report only if Danny's position couldn't be inferred from the player's input or the Warden's response. 
- Then I looked for clear change-of-position indicators in the Warden's response. For example, turn 18-rep 01 begins, "You head back down to the lower deck." Regardless of where Danny started, the Warden knows he's going to the lower deck. 
- Then I judged if that was correct against the initial game state. For turn 18-rep 01, for example, Danny goes to the lower deck to visit Mara's berth, but the initial game state has crew berths on the middle deck. This example is a _fail_ result.
- I judged a rep as _na_ if there were no positional or change-of-position indicators in the Warden's response. In turn 18-rep 04, for example, starts off "You knock twice [on Mara's hatch]." There's no implied change of position, no indication of which deck Mara's berth is on, so I marked it _na_.
- _error_ is not a judgment: the turn threw and there is no narration to mark. All seven in this run are `**Turn threw:**` reps.
- _pass_ is what is left: the response has at least one positional indicator and none of them contradicts the initial game state.

#### Where Danny starts *(clarification, 2026-10-07)*

The starting position comes from the session history up to that turn, even where
the history is wrong. From there, the route the rep narrates and the deck it names
or implies for the *destination* must both match the seeded layout. When the rep
does not name the destination's deck, work it out from the history's start.

This is the one place where earlier narration is accepted over the seeded facts.
A rep is not failed for a wrong start it inherited, only for what it narrates
from there.

| Case | Start, per the history | Against the seeded layout |
|---|---|---|
| turn 08 | the bridge, upper deck | correct |
| turn 14 | the corridor outside the cryo bay, mid-deck | correct |
| turn 18 | the cryo bay bulkhead, mid-deck | correct |
| turn 24 | Mara's berth, which turns 14 and 18 put on the lower deck | wrong: berths are on mid-deck |
| turn 29 | the mess hall, mid-deck | correct |

Worked examples:

- Turn 18 rep 01 goes down from mid-deck to reach the berths. The destination is
  on the wrong deck: _fail_.
- Turn 24 rep 03 says the mess is one deck up from Mara's berth. From the
  history's start on the lower deck, that is mid-deck: _pass_, although it
  repeats the inherited error about the berth.
- Turn 24 rep 02 names the destination outright as mid-deck, a short climb away:
  _pass_. The narration ends up back in the right place without saying so.
- Turn 24 rep 07 says the mess is two decks up, which is the upper deck: _fail_.
- A turn 24 rep saying only "Teo's bunk is just along this deck" would be a
  _fail_: from the history's start that is the lower deck, and all berths are on
  mid-deck. No rep in the 2026-10-04 run did this.
- A turn 24 rep that corrects the record out loud would be a _pass_: one saying
  the berths were on mid-deck all along and the earlier narration had them
  wrong, then placing Teo's bunk along the same deck. It names the destination's
  deck, as rep 02 does, and the deck is right. What separates it from the fail
  above is that mid-deck is named on the page. No rep in the 2026-10-04 run did
  this. *(2026-10-08)*
- Turn 14 rep 01 goes "past mid-deck" from a mid-deck start. The destination
  (cargo bay, lower deck) is right, but the route is impossible on the layout:
  _fail_.
